Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

UNISONE (“we”, “us”, “the app”) is a free web app made by Pallab Ghosh in India. It lets friends listen to the same YouTube song at the same moment. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We follow applicable Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. What we collect

  • The name you type. It is shown to the other people in your room (up to 20 characters).
  • An anonymous ID. Firebase creates a random ID for your device so that the app knows who is who. There is no email, phone number or password.
  • Room information. The room code, the list of people in the room, the song queue (YouTube video IDs, titles and channel names), playback position, the host’s settings, join requests, and the time someone was last in the room.
  • Information saved on your device. Your name, the room you are in, and a small timing measurement that helps keep playback in sync.
  • What you search for. The words you type are sent to our server so that it can ask YouTube for results. Results may be kept for up to 24 hours to save quota.
  • Technical data. Our hosting and network providers process your IP address and basic browser details in order to deliver the site.

We do not collect your email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone, or payment details. There are no ads, no analytics trackers, and we never sell your information.

2. Why we use it

To run rooms, keep playback in sync, show who is in a room, let you search for songs, keep the service secure and prevent misuse, and make it more reliable.

3. Services we rely on

  • Firebase (Google): anonymous sign-in and the realtime database. Firebase privacy information
  • Cloudflare Workers: our search and clock service.
  • Vercel: hosting of the website.
  • Google Fonts: the fonts used on the site.
  • YouTube: songs play through the YouTube player and search uses the YouTube Data API. YouTube and Google may collect data (such as cookies, IP address and viewing activity) under the Google Privacy Policy. By using UNISONE you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.

4. Who can see what

People in the same room can see your name and the songs in the queue. Anyone who has a room code can ask to join; the host can approve people, lock the room or remove someone. Please do not use your real full name, phone number or any private details as your display name.

5. How long we keep it

When a host ends a room, its data is deleted. Rooms that are simply left behind may stay stored until we remove them. You can ask us to delete anything linked to you (see section 11). Clearing the site’s data in your browser removes what is saved on your device.

6. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw your consent, and have a complaint heard. Write to us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Children

If you are under 18, please use UNISONE with the permission of a parent or guardian. We do not track or profile children, we show no ads, and the only personal detail we handle is a display name.

8. Security

The site uses HTTPS and the database has access rules that limit what each person can do. No online service can be completely secure, so please use the app with that in mind.

9. Where data is processed

Our providers may process data on servers located outside India.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
